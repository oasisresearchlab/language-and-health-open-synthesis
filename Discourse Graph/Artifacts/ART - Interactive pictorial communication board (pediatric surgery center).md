## Purpose

An interactive pictorial communication board deployed in a pediatric ambulatory surgery center to let bedside nurses convey routine peri-operative nursing tasks to limited-English-proficiency (LEP) patients and caregivers in real time, reserving the telephone interpreting system for more in-depth conversations (e.g., pre-operative care, discharge instructions). It targets the top five LEP languages at the site (Spanish, Burmese, Portuguese, Vietnamese, French).

> "Bedside nurses desired a tool to assist with translating commonly performed nursing tasks to their LEP populations in real time, and to reserve the telephone interpreting system for more in-depth conversations such as pre-operative care and post-operative discharge instructions." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 20)

## Mechanism

The board uses standard pictographic symbols (chosen per graphic-design guidance to be simple and concrete) so a nurse or patient can point to an icon representing a task/item; a template was adapted and professionally translated into the five target languages and reviewed by bedside nurses for comprehensibility (Figure 2).

> "Taking these principles into consideration, standard symbols were chosen to represent each individual task/item included on the communication board. These symbols were then reviewed by bedside nurses to ensure understanding." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 20)

> "A communication board template was then adapted and translated by LATN Language Solutions (Atlanta, GA) into the top five identified languages: Spanish, Burmese, Portuguese, Vietnamese, and French (Figure 2)." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 20)

## Example

Implemented in the pre- and post-operative units of a three-OR ambulatory surgery center at a large, urban pediatric healthcare system; used specifically with LEP caregivers in the pre- and post-operative settings, where 11% of 2018 patients identified as LEP.

> "The communication board was specifically utilized with LEP caregivers in the pre- and post-operative settings." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 20)
