## Limitation

The app ease-of-use and satisfaction ratings come from a survey the authors describe as exploratory: although based on the System Usability Scale, the items were simplified for readability and the instrument's validity was not assessed. The median ratings should therefore be read as indicative appraisal signals from a brief hands-on demonstration, not validated usability measurements.

## Supporting Quote

> "While the surveys used in this study were based on the system usability scale, with questions simplified to enhance readability, they were ultimately designed to be explorative and we did not assess their validity." (Panayiotou, 2020, p. 3524)
