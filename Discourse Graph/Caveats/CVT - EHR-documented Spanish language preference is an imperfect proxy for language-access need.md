## Limitation

The cohort was defined by patients' documented Spanish-language preference in the EHR, which the authors note is an imperfect indicator of actual language-access need. Some flagged patients may have preferred English ("false positives") and some who needed interpreters may have identified as English-speaking ("false negatives") out of fear of bias or embarrassment, so the denominators and the measured proportions of concordant-care provision may be mis-estimated.

## Supporting Quote

> "Identiﬁcation of Spanish-speaking patient charts relied on the patients' documented language preferences in the EHR, which is an imperfect indicator for language access needs. Some patients with a designated Spanish language preference may have preferred to speak in English with their care team (a 'false positive'), and other patients may have preferred interpreter services but chosen to identify as English speaking (a 'false negative')." (Jensen, 2023, p. 436)
