## Limitation

The null finding of equal visit length across concordance groups must be interpreted with caution: the discordant-visit duration includes minutes when the interpreter (rather than the patient or physician) was speaking. Equal total length therefore likely conceals that patients and physicians were actually speaking less during interpreter-mediated visits, meaning direct patient–provider communication was reduced even though the clock time was comparable.

## Supporting Quote

> "Although we observed no difference in length of visit for Spanish-discordant patients, this result must be interpreted with caution. Because the visit included minutes when the interpreter was speaking, the equal length of visit we observed likely indicates that patients and physicians were actually speaking less during the Spanish-discordant interviews, decreasing patient–provider communication further." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 673)
