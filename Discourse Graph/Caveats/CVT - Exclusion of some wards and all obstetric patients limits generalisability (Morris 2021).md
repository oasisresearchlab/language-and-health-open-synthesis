## Limitation

The sample excluded some hospital wards and all obstetric patients (audited post-admission and therefore outside the inclusion criteria), as well as non-admitted (outpatient/ED ambulatory) patients. The authors note this limits the generalisability of the findings, so the utilisation rate and characteristics observed may not represent the full LEP inpatient population of the district.

## Supporting Quote

> "Second, the sample did not include some hospital wards (due to exclusion criteria) and all obstetric patients were audited post admission and, therefore, excluded from the study, thereby limiting generalisability of findings." (Morris, 2021, p. 430)
