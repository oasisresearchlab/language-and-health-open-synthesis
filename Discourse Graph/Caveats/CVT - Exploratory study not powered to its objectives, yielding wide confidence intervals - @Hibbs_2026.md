## Limitation

This was an exploratory study with no pre-existing data on expected SACT-comprehension rates in this population, so no formal sample-size calculation was possible and the sample was set by available time, funding and community-partner capacity. Consequently the study was not definitively powered to its objectives and the effect-size estimates carry wide confidence intervals — the odds ratios and regression coefficients are imprecise and should be treated as hypothesis-generating rather than definitive.
