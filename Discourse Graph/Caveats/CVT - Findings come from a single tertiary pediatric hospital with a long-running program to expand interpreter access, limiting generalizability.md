## Limitation

All data come from a single tertiary-care referral pediatric hospital (Seattle Children's Hospital) that had already invested in a multi-year institutional program to expand interpreter access, universal language-needs screening, and a twice-daily interpreted-care target. Both the observed LEP–EP disparities and the interpretation dose-response may differ at institutions with weaker interpreter infrastructure or different case mixes, so the single-center setting limits generalizability of the effect sizes.

## Supporting Quote

> "The study was conducted in a tertiary care referral pediatric hospital." (Jimenez, 2014, p. 2)
