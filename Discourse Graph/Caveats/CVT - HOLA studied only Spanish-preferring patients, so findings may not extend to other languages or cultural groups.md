## Limitation

The study focused on a single LEP group — patients identifying Spanish as their preferred language. The authors caution that whether the findings extend to other languages or to different ethnic or cultural groups is unknown and deserves further study.

## Supporting Quote

> "Additionally, we focused on a group of patients identiﬁed by their preferred language of Spanish, and whether these ﬁndings extend to other languages or different ethnic or cultural groups deserves further study." (Seible, 2021, p. 860)
