## Limitation

Because the communication modality cannot be hidden, the trial was necessarily conducted unblinded. The authors acknowledge that patients' and physicians' awareness of study-arm allocation could have introduced bias into the patient-reported satisfaction outcomes.

## Supporting Quote

> "Furthermore, because the study by necessity was conducted in an unblinded fashion, it is possible that awareness of study arm allocation introduced bias." (Seible, 2021, p. 860)
