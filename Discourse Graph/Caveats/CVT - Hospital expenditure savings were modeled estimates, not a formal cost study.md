## Limitation

The reported hospital expenditure savings are modeled estimates derived from averted readmissions, not the product of a formal cost study. The figure depends on attributing the observed readmission decline to the intervention and on the held-constant preintervention rate assumption, so the $161,404 monthly savings should be read as an illustrative projection rather than a measured cost outcome.

## Supporting Quote

> "Finally, we did not conduct a formal cost study, but rather only were able to estimate potential expenditure savings associated with the lower rate of readmission during the intervention period." (Karliner, 2017, p. 206)
