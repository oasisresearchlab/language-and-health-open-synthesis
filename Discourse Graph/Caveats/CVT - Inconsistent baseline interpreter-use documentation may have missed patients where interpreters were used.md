## Limitation

Baseline interpreter use was reconstructed from a manual chart review, but documentation of interpreter use was inconsistent before the project, so patients who had an interpreter but no documentation may have been missed. This biases the 35.7% baseline AIUD rate downward and inflates the apparent magnitude of the improvement to 64.5%, since part of the measured gain could be documentation catching up rather than new interpreter use.

## Supporting Quote

> "To determine our baseline interpreter use, a time-intensive and thorough manual chart review was performed. Documentation of interpreter use was inconsistent and may have resulted in missing patients where interpreters were used." (Martinez, 2021, p. 7)
