## Limitation

The scenario-based mode-of-consent findings (including reported willingness to use minors and the effect of interpreter wait time) come from surgeons' reactions to hypothetical LEP patient vignettes, not from observed consent encounters. What surgeons say they "would" do in a constructed scenario may not match what they actually do at the bedside, so these prevalence figures should be read as reported intentions under hypothetical conditions.

## Supporting Quote

> "Even with this guidance, surgeons in our study reported that they would use minors as interpreters in hypothetical situations to obtain informed consent." (Patel, 2016, p. 520)
