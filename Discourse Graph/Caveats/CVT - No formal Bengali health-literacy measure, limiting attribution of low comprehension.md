## Limitation

The study did not include a formal, validated measurement of health literacy in Bengali. Because health literacy was not measured, the low comprehension observed after reading the translated booklet cannot be cleanly attributed to translation quality versus underlying health literacy or other communication barriers — limiting the interpretation that translated written information alone is the binding constraint.

## Supporting Quote

> "Fourth, we did not include a formal measurement of health literacy in Bengali [34]; this limits our ability to assess the contribution of health literacy to our study endpoints." (Hibbs, 2026, p. 7)
