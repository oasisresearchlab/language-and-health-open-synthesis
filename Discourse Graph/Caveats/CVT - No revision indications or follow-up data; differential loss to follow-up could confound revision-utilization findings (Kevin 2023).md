## Limitation

The dataset did not include indications for revision surgery, so the reasons LEP versus EP patients did or did not undergo revision cannot be characterized, and the database could not distinguish whether the lower revision rate among LEP patients reflected reduced access versus differential loss to follow-up. The authors state a more granular assessment of frequency and duration of follow-up care is needed to explain the findings. This leaves open a non-access explanation for the observed decreased-utilization signal.

## Supporting Quote

> "Another limitation is that this study's dataset did not include indications for revision surgeries." (Kevin, 2023, p. 1432)

> "Also, our study's database did not allow us to investigate whether differential rates of revision surgeries may be related to loss to follow-up. To further strengthen and explain the findings of this study, a more granular assessment of frequency and duration of follow-up care is needed." (Kevin, 2023, p. 1432)
