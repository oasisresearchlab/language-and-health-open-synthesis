## Limitation

Only English-to-Spanish written translation was evaluated. Spanish is a high-resource language for which the machine-translation engine had abundant parallel training text; usefulness and error rates in other languages — especially lower-resource languages with sparser training corpora — were not assessed and could be substantially worse. The finding that machine translation produced no more clinically dangerous errors than professional translation should not be generalized to other language pairs.

## Supporting Quote

> "Second, we only evaluated GT for English translated into Spanish; its usefulness in other languages will need to be evaluated." (Khanna, 2011, p. 524)
