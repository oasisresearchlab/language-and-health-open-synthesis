## Limitation

Adherence was measured indirectly from pharmacy refill data (the Adherence Ratio), a surrogate chosen because no validated direct metric was available. This captures whether medication was *obtained*, not whether it was actually *taken* correctly — a construct-validity gap. The authors also note patients may have filled prescriptions at unlisted pharmacies, undercounting fills.

## Supporting Quote

> "the usefulness of the metrics may be limited in that they do not assess how administration of medication occurs once the patient has the medicine in hand, or any other more qualitative factor in the use of the drug." (Kahler, 2022, p. 35)

> "it is conceivable that they were using other, unlisted pharmacies without the awareness of the clinical team." (Kahler, 2022, p. 36)
