## Limitation

The qualitative evidence on interpreters' expanded care-coordination role comes from postintervention observations conducted by the program administrator who led the restructuring [L.M.] and from interviews by a research assistant, with no preintervention qualitative baseline and no independent or blinded analysis reported. Insider-conducted, postintervention-only qualitative data are prone to favorable framing and confirmation bias, so the described role expansion and communication improvements should be read as supportive illustration rather than independently validated outcomes.
