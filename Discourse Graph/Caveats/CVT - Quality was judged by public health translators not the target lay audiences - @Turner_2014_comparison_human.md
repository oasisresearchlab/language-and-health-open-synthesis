## Limitation

The quality equivalence was established by public health professionals who were themselves involved in translation activities, not by members of the lay, limited-English-proficient audiences for whom the materials are intended. Professional translators may weigh linguistic fidelity differently from how a lay reader experiences comprehensibility, so equivalent expert preference does not guarantee equivalent real-world understanding by the target readers.

## Supporting Quote

> "The quality analysis was performed with public health professionals involved in translation activities; however, they were not performed using the target audiences." (Turner, 2014, p. 528)
