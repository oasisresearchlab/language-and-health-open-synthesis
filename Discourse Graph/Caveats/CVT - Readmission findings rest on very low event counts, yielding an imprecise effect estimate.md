## Limitation

The readmission findings rest on very few events. With readmission rates of 0.96% among 313 ES (≈3 patients) and 4.43% among 158 NES (≈7 patients), both the univariate significance test and the multivariable odds ratio are driven by roughly a dozen readmissions across the whole cohort. This is reflected in the wide 95% confidence interval for the multivariable estimate (OR 4.22, 95% CI 1.63–10.94), which signals substantial imprecision. The authors separately acknowledge that the overall NES sample was small.

## Supporting Quote

> "Second, our sample size is relatively small. Over the 6-year period queried, only 220 patients were NES." (Tejas, 2025, p. E559)

> "On multivariable analysis, NES status was an independent predictor of increased readmission [OR = 4.22 (1.63–10.94), P < 0.001; Table 4]." (Tejas, 2025, p. E557)
