## Limitation

The $5,847,000 figure is the total campus-wide language-services cost for 2016, not a cost specific to the surgical/procedural cohort that the study actually observed. The authors state that only a fraction of this amount was directly attributable to the surveyed practice areas, so the cost cannot be read as the price of interpretive services for the surgical population studied. The figures also come from a descriptive accounting-datasheet review, not a formal economic evaluation.

## Supporting Quote

> "While only a fraction of this cost was directly attributable to patients visiting the surgical and procedural practice areas we surveyed in our study, the availability of in person interpreters to meet the needs of all our patients requires a total of 43 full time employee equivalent interpreters." (Burkle, 2017, p. 4)
