## Limitation

All effectiveness outcomes are nurse self-reports on Likert surveys and are subject to social-desirability bias: nurses may have felt obligated to report improvement after the intervention. The authors themselves flag the striking contrast between the highly positive survey responses and the low actual board utilization as a signal that the satisfaction gains may be inflated.

## Supporting Quote

> "In addition, social desirability bias may have influenced results given the contrast between the positive survey responses and low board utilization rate." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 24)

> "First, it is possible that nurses felt obligated to report improvements related to implementation of the communication board." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 24)
