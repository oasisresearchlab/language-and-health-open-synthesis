## Limitation

The self-report recall window (7 days) differed from the claims-based CMG capture period (1 year), which can attenuate convergent validity (and may explain the non-significant diabetes-pill gradient). CMG was not calculable for some participants, the statistical comparison is conservative, prescribing data could bias CMG, and the sample is subject to selection bias with limited generalizability; the study was underpowered for subgroup analyses.

## Supporting Quote
