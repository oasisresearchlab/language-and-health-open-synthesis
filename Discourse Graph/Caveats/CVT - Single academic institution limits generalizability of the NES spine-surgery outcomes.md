## Limitation

The study was conducted at a single large academic institution (Hospital for Special Surgery, New York). The authors caution that their NES population may not represent NES spine-surgery patients across the United States, whose demographics vary substantially by geography. A majority of NES patients were coded as "other" race in the EMR, so residual baseline differences may persist despite matching. Findings therefore may not generalize to other settings, payer mixes, or language populations.

## Supporting Quote

> "First, given the retrospective nature of a single academic institution, there exists a concern about the generalizability of this study." (Tejas, 2025, p. E559)

> "Overall, patients at our institution, and more specifically, NES patients at our institution, may not be representative of patients undergoing spine surgery across the United States." (Tejas, 2025, p. E559)
