## Limitation

The study was conducted only at academic teaching hospitals where all Spanish speakers received adequate interpretive services, which the authors note may not reflect results at other types of clinics — especially those without adequate interpretive services. The null finding on interpretive-service type is therefore specific to a setting where baseline interpretation was already adequate, and may not generalize to settings where interpretation is scarce or absent. Colposcopy is also a relatively simple procedure, so conclusions may not extend to more complex medical information where language could matter more.

## Supporting Quote

> "This study was done at academic teaching hospitals, which may not reflect results at other types of clinics, especially those without adequate interpretive services." (Krankl, 2011, p. 84)

> "Colposcopy is a relatively simple procedure; thus, our conclusions may not apply to more complicated medical information, where language differences may play a greater role." (Krankl, 2011, p. 85)
