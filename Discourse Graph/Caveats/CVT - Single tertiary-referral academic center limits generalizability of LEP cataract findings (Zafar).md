## Limitation

The study drew from a single academic tertiary-referral eye center whose LEP population may differ from LEP patients elsewhere. Its LEP rate (6.4%) was below the metro Denver average, which the authors attribute to access barriers under-representing LEP patients and to the center serving patients from outside the metro area. The authors explicitly caution that this specific LEP group may not generalize to other practice settings.

## Supporting Quote

> "Our cohort has a lower LEP rate than that of metro Denver; we believe this is due to barriers to healthcare access which cause LEP patients to be underrepresented in the medical system. In addition, our institution is a tertiary referral center; thus, we serve patients who live outside the metro Denver area. Thus, it must also be mentioned that the specific group of LEP patients at our academic institution may not be generalizable to LEP patients in other practice settings." (Zafar, 2023, p. 600)
