## Limitation

All participants were pediatric surgery patients at a single institution in urban Northern California, whose clinician and patient demographics may differ from other settings, and the small, single-hospital sample may not represent the diversity of the Spanish-speaking population. These features constrain external validity: the observed concordance effects may not generalize to other surgical subspecialties, geographic regions, LEP language groups, or health systems.

## Supporting Quote

> "Second, our population only included pediatric surgery patients at one institution. This institution as well as our geographic location in urban Northern California may have different clinician and patient demographics than other locations, which could affect the patient–provider interactions we observed." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 673)

> "The diversity of the Spanish-speaking population may not be well-represented in our small study at one hospital." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 674)
