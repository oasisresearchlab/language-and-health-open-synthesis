## Limitation

The study is a single-center, retrospective observational cohort drawn from one urban tertiary-care children's hospital with a specific (majority Hispanic/Medicaid) population, so its findings may not generalize to other settings. LEP status was ascertained by chart review (consent-form language and interpreter-use documentation), which the authors note is subject to misclassification in both directions. Because the design is observational, the association between LEP and delayed analgesia cannot be interpreted as causal.

## Supporting Quote

> "Our study was observational and has several limitations. First, misclassification was possible in that some patients who were in LEP families may have been documented as English proficient and vice versa. Next, the study was conducted in a single center with a specific population and may not be generalizable." (Plancarte, 2021, p. 1203)

> "This was a retrospective cohort study." (Plancarte, 2021, p. 1200)
