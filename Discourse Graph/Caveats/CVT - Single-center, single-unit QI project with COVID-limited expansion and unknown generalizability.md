## Limitation

The interpreter-usage findings come from a single quality improvement project conducted on one perianesthesia unit at a single cancer center (MSKCC). The task force had planned to extend the interventions to the hospital's other medical, surgical, inpatient, and outpatient units, but that expansion was curtailed by the COVID-19 pandemic (remote LAP staff, nurse redeployment, and paused education sessions), and the authors state it is unknown whether the observed usage data would have been reproduced on other units. Generalizability beyond this single unit and institution is therefore unestablished.

## Supporting Quote

> "This quality improvement project had several limitations. The project was conducted on a perianesthesia unit at a single cancer center. Although the task force planned to implement the project's interventions on the medical and surgical inpatient and outpatient units to improve language access for the entire hospital, further expansion of these interventions to other units was limited because of the COVID-19 pandemic. It is unknown whether the data presented herein would have been duplicated on other units." (Linda, 2023, p. 362)
