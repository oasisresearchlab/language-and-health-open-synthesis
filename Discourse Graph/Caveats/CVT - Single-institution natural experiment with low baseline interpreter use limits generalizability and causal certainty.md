## Limitation

The study was a single-institution natural experiment with limited baseline interpreter use, and it included only patients aged 50 years and older. As a quasi-experimental design at one hospital, it cannot fully rule out secular trends or unmeasured confounding (the authors note they cannot explain the concurrent rise in EP readmission), and the readmission benefit may not generalize to hospitals serving younger LEP populations or with different baseline interpreter access.

## Supporting Quote

> "Our study only encompassed the experience of a single institution with limited interpreter use in the hospital before starting the intervention. ... Finally, our study included only patients 50 years and older ... our results may not be as generalizable to hospitals serving a younger LEP population." (Karliner, 2017, p. 206)
