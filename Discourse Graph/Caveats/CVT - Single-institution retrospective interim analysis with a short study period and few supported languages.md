## Limitation

The findings come from a single urban academic medical center, are retrospective, cover only a short study period (an interim analysis, Feb 8 – Jun 14 2023), and involve a limited number of languages with translated written consent templates (five). These features limit generalizability of the post-implementation concordance rates and the language/service/urgency subgroup patterns to other settings, later time points, and other language groups.

## Supporting Quote

> "Limitations of this study include the single-institution and retrospective nature, having a limited number of supported languages, and a short study period in this interim analysis." (Trang, 2024, p. 531)
