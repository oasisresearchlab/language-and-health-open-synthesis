## Limitation

The audit sample was relatively small (90 episodes / 74 patients), and the authors caution it may not have provided sufficient statistical power to detect true differences in length of stay or cost. The non-significant casemix-adjusted LOS and cost findings therefore cannot be read as evidence of no effect — particularly given that the unadjusted-outcome multivariate models did reach significance, and that the point estimates were in the direction of longer, costlier stays for interpreter recipients.

## Supporting Quote

> "First, while it was possible to estimate the cost and LOS associated with use of interpreting services, the sample size was relatively small and may not have provided sufficient power to detect true differences." (Morris, 2021, p. 430)
