## Limitation

The perception findings come from a small convenience sample drawn from only two older migrant communities (Greek and Chinese, n = 12), recruited through ethno-specific organisations, plus nursing/allied-health staff (n = 17) working only in sub-acute aged-care settings. The authors note this limits generalisability to other migrant communities and other areas of health care, and that the small samples precluded evaluating differences between cultural groups or professions. Perceptions are therefore contextual to these groups and settings rather than broadly representative.

## Supporting Quote

> "Firstly, we only conducted consultations with two older migrant communities of Australia, and subsequently, the findings from our focus groups and surveys may lack generalisability to other older migrant communities of Australia." (Panayiotou, 2020, p. 3524)

> "In addition, we recruited through ethno-specific organisations and thus may have an unrepresentative sample of participants accessing community services." (Panayiotou, 2020, p. 3524)
