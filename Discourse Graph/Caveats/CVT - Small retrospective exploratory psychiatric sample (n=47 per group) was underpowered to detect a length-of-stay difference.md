## Limitation

This was a retrospective, exploratory study with 47 patients per group. The authors note the limited number of cases reduces statistical power, so the non-significant length-of-stay difference (median 12 vs 8 days, p=0.155) cannot be read as evidence of no effect — the study was likely underpowered to detect the difference it was designed to find.

## Supporting Quote

> "These findings should be interpreted cautiously as this was a retrospective and exploratory study with a number of limitations. Firstly, there were a limited number of cases examined which reduces the statistical power." (Daly, 2019, p. 467)
