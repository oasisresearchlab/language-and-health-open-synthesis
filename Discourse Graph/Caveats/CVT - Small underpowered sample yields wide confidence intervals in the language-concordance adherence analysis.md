## Limitation

The study analyzed only 59 patients, split across three language groups and further across medication subsamples (as small as n=2 in some Table 3 cells). The authors state the language-concordance analysis was underpowered, producing wide confidence intervals — so the concordance findings, while suggestive, are statistically fragile.

## Supporting Quote

> [!info]
> "The study was underpowered, leading to wide confidence intervals in the language concordance analysis." (Kahler, 2022, p. 35)
>
> "This study was limited by the small number of participants." (Kahler, 2022, p. 35)
