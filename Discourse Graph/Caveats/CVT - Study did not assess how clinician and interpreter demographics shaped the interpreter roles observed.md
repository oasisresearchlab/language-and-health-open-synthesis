## Limitation

The authors state it was outside the scope of the study to assess how clinician and interpreter demographic characteristics influenced the roles interpreters took on. Because all interpreters were female and most registrars were female and white, the role dynamics observed may be confounded by these characteristics, and the study cannot attribute the observed roles specifically to the interpreting arrangement versus participant demographics.

## Supporting Quote

> "It should be noted that it is not within the scope of our study to assess the influence of clinician and interpreter demographic characteristics on the roles taken on by interpreters." (Kilian, 2021, p. 35)
