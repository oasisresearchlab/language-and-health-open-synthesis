## Limitation

The US$1,498-per-translation figure is a simplified single-centre estimate: it assumes every study translated only the initial consent document, at a flat 20 cents per word (the centre's median rate over the period). Real per-trial costs vary, and additional translation costs are incurred at each protocol amendment — so the estimate is a lower-bound, centre-specific approximation rather than a generalizable cost.

## Supporting Quote

> "For simplicity, we assumed that every study had the initial consent document translated at 20 cents per word, the median cost for translation paid by the cancer centre during the evaluated period (Supplementary Methods)." (Maria, 2023, Methods)

> "Additional costs would be incurred to translate a consent document at the time of protocol amendments, an amount that would vary by trial." (Maria, 2023, p. 856)
