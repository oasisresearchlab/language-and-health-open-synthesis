## Limitation

This is an uncontrolled, single-site quality-improvement (pre-post) report from one hospital with no concurrent control group, no statistical testing of the pre-post differences, and outcomes measured over a multi-year period of concurrent organizational change (CEO sponsorship, staff turnover, broader restructuring). The reported complaint reductions and satisfaction increase cannot be causally attributed to the career ladder alone, and the percentage reductions derive from very small daily complaint counts (e.g., 8→2, 1→0, 6→1). The authors themselves flag that care outcomes and cost-effectiveness were not yet evaluated, underscoring that these are descriptive QI signals rather than effect estimates.
