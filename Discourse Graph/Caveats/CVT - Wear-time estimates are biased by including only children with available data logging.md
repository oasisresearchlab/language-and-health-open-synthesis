## Limitation

The wear-time estimates include only children who returned for a follow-up visit and for whom data logging was available; children fit with hearing aids who never returned, or who were recommended hearing aids but never returned for a fitting, are excluded. Because LEP families are generally less likely to access health care services, this selection likely biases the wear-time estimates (probably toward overestimating use among LEP children, since the most disengaged families never appear in the data).

## Supporting Quote

> "The estimates of hearing aid wear times in the current study are biased by including only children whose data logging information was available. Because children and families with limited English proficiency are less likely to access health care services generally (Smith, 2009), it is necessary to expand the scope of this work to include estimates of hearing aid nonuse among children who are deaf or hard of hearing in families with and without limited English proficiency." (Brigham, 2024, p. 327)
