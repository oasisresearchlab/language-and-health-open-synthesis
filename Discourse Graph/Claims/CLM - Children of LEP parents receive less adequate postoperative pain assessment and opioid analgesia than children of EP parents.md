Children of parents with limited English proficiency (LEP) receive less adequate postoperative pain care than children of English-proficient parents: they are assessed for pain less often and receive opioid analgesics only at higher levels of recorded pain.

## Supporting Evidence

> [!info] EVDs that support this claim (→ EVD—supports→CLM edges by sync_relations.py).

- [[EVD - Children of LEP parents received fewer daily pain assessments than children of EP parents (7.3 vs 9.3 per day) - @Jimenez_2014_Postoperative_pain]]
- [[EVD - Children of LEP parents had higher recorded pain before receiving opioid analgesics than children of EP parents - @Jimenez_2014_Postoperative_pain]]
