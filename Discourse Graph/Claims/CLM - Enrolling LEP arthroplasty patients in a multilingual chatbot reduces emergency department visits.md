## Narrative synthesis

The single study bearing on this claim (Rainey 2023) found a lower ED-visit rate in the chatbot arm (0.9% vs 8.0%) that did not reach statistical significance (P = .085). Per the corpus convention, this non-significant result is wired as *contradicting* rather than supporting, though the point estimate directionally favors the chatbot. Treat as underpowered/hypothesis-generating pending a properly powered comparison.
