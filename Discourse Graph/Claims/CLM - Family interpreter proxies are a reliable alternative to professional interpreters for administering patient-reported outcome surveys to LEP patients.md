Family or carer interpreter proxies produce patient-reported outcome survey results that agree closely enough with those obtained through certified professional healthcare interpreters to be a reliable, lower-cost alternative for routine post-discharge surveillance of LEP patients, with negligible systematic bias at the group level.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Interpreter proxies and healthcare interpreters showed substantial-to-almost-perfect agreement across most arthroplasty PROMs - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]
- [[EVD - Oxford hip-knee score showed almost perfect proxy-interpreter agreement (kappa-ICC 0.87, CCC 0.86) after arthroplasty - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]
- [[EVD - EQ-VAS overall-health score showed substantial proxy-interpreter agreement (ICC-CCC 0.78) after arthroplasty - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]
- [[EVD - Satisfaction and success PROMs showed substantial proxy-interpreter agreement (kappa 0.75 and 0.70) after arthroplasty - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]
- [[EVD - Readmission and reoperation responses showed perfect proxy-interpreter agreement (kappa 1.00) after arthroplasty - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]
- [[EVD - Mean differences between proxy and interpreter scores were small with negligible group-level bias and no significant Wilcoxon differences - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]

## Contradicting Evidence

- [[EVD - Stratified by interview order only EQ-VAS with healthcare-interpreter-first differed significantly between methods (p 0.04) - @Xue_2019_Interpreter_proxy]]
