A system intervention that increases rapid, immediate access to professional interpreters (such as bedside interpreter phones) improves patient-reported informed consent for hospitalized patients with limited English proficiency undergoing invasive procedures.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Adequately informed consent was higher post- vs pre-bedside interpreter phone implementation (54% vs 29%) among LEP patients - @Lee_2017_Increased_Access]]
- [[EVD - Post-implementation LEP patients had higher propensity-adjusted odds of adequately informed consent (AOR 2.56) - @Lee_2017_Increased_Access]]
- [[EVD - Each individual informed consent element improved post-implementation among LEP patients (adjusted ORs 2.39-14.1) - @Lee_2017_Increased_Access]]
