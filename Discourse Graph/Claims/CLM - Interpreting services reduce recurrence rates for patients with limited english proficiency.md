  > *The evidence suggests higher emergency department presentations for EP (English Proficient) patients compared to LEP (Limited English Proficiency) patients, which implies LEP patients may have less healthcare engagement. This contradicts the claim that LEP patients have lower recurrence likelihood when interpreting services are used.*
- [[@Barwise_2018_Differences_code#^Barwise-2018-Differe-L9]] (confidence: 0.90)
