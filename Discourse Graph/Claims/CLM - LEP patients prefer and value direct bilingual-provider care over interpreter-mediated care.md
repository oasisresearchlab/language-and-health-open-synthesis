LEP (Spanish-speaking) patients and their families express a strong preference for, and place high value on, direct language-concordant care from a bilingual provider over interpreter-mediated care; they endorse that discordant care worsens quality and report less comfort using an interpreter.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Both language concordance and speaking Spanish predicted greater stated preference for language-concordant care (p=0.03 and p less than 0.001) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
- [[EVD - Concordant and Spanish-speaking families more strongly agreed that discordance worsens quality of care (beta=-0.46, p=0.01) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
- [[EVD - Spanish-speaking families reported less comfort using an interpreter (beta=-0.659, p=0.005) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
