## Narrative synthesis

This is the **exposure-contrast** claim (discordant/LEP care vs concordant/English-proficient care), distinct from the intervention claims about whether interpreter availability or staffing investment changes provider burden. The self-reported signal is graded by linguistic distance: providers rate effort highest for non-English/non-Spanish speakers, intermediate for Spanish speakers, consistent with a confidence/effort gradient (Aparna 2025). The contradicting cohort study shows the limit of the claim — LEP psychiatric inpatients accrued more consultant reviews in absolute terms, but the excess disappeared once normalized per length-of-stay day, suggesting the extra effort tracked longer admissions rather than a higher per-encounter burden (Daly 2019). The supporting evidence is provider self-report rather than a measured time outcome, so the claim is held at moderate strength pending objective time-motion measurement.
