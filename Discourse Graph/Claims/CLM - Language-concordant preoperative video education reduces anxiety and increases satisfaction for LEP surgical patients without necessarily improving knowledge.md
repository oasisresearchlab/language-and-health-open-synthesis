Adding language-concordant preoperative video education (an instructional anesthesia video in the patient's native language) to an interpreter-assisted preanesthesia interview reduces anxiety and increases satisfaction for limited-English-proficiency surgical patients, without necessarily improving their self-reported knowledge.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Spanish instructional video reduced preoperative anxiety vs no video (median reduction 2 vs 0) in Spanish-speaking surgical patients - @West_2014_effects_preoperative]]
- [[EVD - Spanish instructional video increased preoperative satisfaction vs no video (median increase 2 vs 0) in Spanish-speaking surgical patients - @West_2014_effects_preoperative]]
- [[EVD - Spanish instructional video did not improve reported knowledge scores vs no video (3.5 vs 4, P=.908) in Spanish-speaking surgical patients - @West_2014_effects_preoperative]]
