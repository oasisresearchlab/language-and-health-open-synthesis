Greater availability of language-concordant patient information and interpreters for a given language mitigates the surgical-access disadvantage of limited English proficiency for speakers of that language. The commentary offers this as the mechanistic explanation for why Spanish-speaking LEP patients — served by relatively abundant Spanish-language resources — showed no reduced odds of parathyroidectomy, whereas non-Spanish LEP patients did.
