## Narrative synthesis

Of the review's 4 studies evaluating surgical-care delays, 2 reported significant delays for patients with LEP, 1 reported no difference, and 1 was equivocal (significant delay in the more recent subcohort but not the historical subcohort). The abstract summarizes this as delays in obtaining care in 3 of 4 studies. Studies defined "delay" heterogeneously (time from diagnosis to surgery, diagnosis to treatment, or referral to surgical-department encounter), so the review reports directionality rather than a pooled estimate. Certainty/GRADE appraisal is left for expert review.
