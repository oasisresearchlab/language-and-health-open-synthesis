Limited English proficiency is associated with reduced access to definitive surgical treatment, with LEP patients less likely than English-proficient patients to undergo guideline-recommended parathyroidectomy for primary hyperparathyroidism. This commentary frames the disparity as a "language justice" problem — limited English proficiency should not equate to limited surgical access.
