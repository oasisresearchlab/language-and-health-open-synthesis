A literal, word-for-word translation of a research instrument creates more comprehension problems for limited-English-proficiency readers than a functionalist (reader-oriented) translation of the same instrument, because the literal version carries source-language structure that reads as awkward and unclear in the target language.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Readers raised significantly more comprehension issues about the literal than the functionalist survey translation - @Colina_2022_Research_Documents]]
- [[EVD - Readers described the functionalist translation as natural and native-authored and the literal one as awkward and foreign - @Colina_2022_Research_Documents]]
