Older people with limited English proficiency and the healthcare workers who care for them regard mobile translation apps as an acceptable and useful adjunct (not a replacement for professional interpreters) for everyday, low-risk healthcare communication.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Older CALD participants and healthcare staff perceived translation technology as having a role in facilitating communication - @Panayiotou_2020_perceptions_translation]]
- [[EVD - Healthcare staff reported already using translation apps on personal devices to overcome language barriers - @Panayiotou_2020_perceptions_translation]]
- [[EVD - Staff felt patients appreciated the effort and it helped build rapport even when the app failed - @Panayiotou_2020_perceptions_translation]]
- [[EVD - Both groups rated the three demonstrated apps as moderately to highly easy to use and satisfactory - @Panayiotou_2020_perceptions_translation]]
