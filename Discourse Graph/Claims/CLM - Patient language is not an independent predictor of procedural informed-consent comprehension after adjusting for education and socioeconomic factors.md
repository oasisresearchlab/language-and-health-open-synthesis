Patient language (English vs Spanish / limited English proficiency) is not an independent predictor of comprehension of procedural informed consent once education and socioeconomic confounders are adjusted for: raw language differences in comprehension attenuate to non-significance after adjustment.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Language was not associated with colposcopy consent comprehension after adjustment (b=-0.44, p=.46) - @Krankl_2011_Patient_predictors]]

## Contradicting Evidence

- [[EVD - English speakers scored higher than Spanish speakers on colposcopy consent comprehension in unadjusted analysis (8.50 vs 6.21) - @Krankl_2011_Patient_predictors]]
