For LEP patients, ratings of trust and (lower) perceived discrimination are associated with the patient's native-language and cultural background — Spanish-speaking families rate these more favorably than English-speaking families — rather than with visit-level language concordance, which is not a significant predictor of either.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - Spanish-speaking families reported higher trust than English-speaking families independent of concordance (beta=0.453, p less than 0.001) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
- [[EVD - Spanish-speaking families reported lower perceived discrimination than English-speaking families independent of concordance (beta=0.255, p=0.002) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
