Differences in patient question-asking are driven by patient–provider language concordance itself, not by the patient's native language, ethnicity, or socioeconomic status: language-concordant patients ask a similar number of questions regardless of whether that shared language is English or Spanish, and native language is not an independent predictor once concordance is modeled.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - English-concordant and Spanish-concordant families asked a similar number of questions (0.65 difference, p=0.9) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
- [[EVD - Language discordance independently predicted fewer patient-initiated questions after adjustment (beta=-2.48, p=0.02) - @Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic]]
