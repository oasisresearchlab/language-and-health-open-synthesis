Surgical patients with limited English proficiency are systematically excluded from, or under-represented in, patient-reported outcome measure (PROM) collection, so their voices are missing from outcomes datasets and value-based care metrics.

- [[EVD - Providers reported either systematic exclusion of LEP patients from PROMs or significant barriers to collection - @Allar_2022_Lost_translation]]
- [[EVD - When translated PROMs were unavailable, PROMs completion rates among LEP patients were lower than English-speaking counterparts - @Allar_2022_Lost_translation]]
