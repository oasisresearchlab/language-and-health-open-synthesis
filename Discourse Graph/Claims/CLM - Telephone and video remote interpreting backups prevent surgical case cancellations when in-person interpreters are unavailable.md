Making telephone and professional video remote interpreting (VRI) available as fallbacks for in-person interpreters keeps interpretive coverage continuous, so that LEP surgical and procedural patients are not delayed, cancelled, or forced to proceed without interpretation when an in-person interpreter is slow or unavailable.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[EVD - No surgical cases were cancelled and no LEP patient underwent a procedure without interpretive assistance - @Burkle_2017_Assessment_efficiency]]
- [[EVD - Telephone and VRI were chosen over in-person interpreters mainly for unstaffed languages long waits or known unavailability - @Burkle_2017_Assessment_efficiency]]
