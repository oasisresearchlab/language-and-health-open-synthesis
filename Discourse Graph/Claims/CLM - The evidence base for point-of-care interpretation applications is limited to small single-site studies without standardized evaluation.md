## Narrative synthesis

The review repeatedly flags the weakness of the evidence base: only eight studies, with small samples (N = 4 to 99), heterogeneous designs, and no standardized methodology for implementing or evaluating the applications, precluding a full quantitative analysis. This is a claim about study quality and evidentiary strength rather than a clinical effect. Certainty/GRADE appraisal is left for expert review.

> "there are few studies with small sample sizes that generalize the usefulness of these tools for LLP patients across the global healthcare setting" (Kiblinger, 2024, p. 4)

> "authors were unable to complete a full analysis due to small sample sizes in the identified articles and no standardized methodology utilized in the implementation and evaluation of the applications" (Kiblinger, 2024, p. 5)
