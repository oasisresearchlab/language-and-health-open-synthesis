## Description

![[Kiblinger_2022_Facilitating_Communication-fig3.png]]

After a 3-month implementation of an interactive pictorial communication board, surgery-center nurses reported a 64% increase in satisfaction with the interpretation process, rising from a mean Likert rating of 2.27 (± 0.88) pre-intervention to 3.57 (± 0.76) post-intervention (p = 0.0007) on a 5-point scale (Figure 3). The change was statistically significant by Wilcoxon rank-sum test.

> "Nurses reported a 64% increase in satisfaction with the interpretation process (2.27 ± 0.88 pre; 3.57 ± 0.76 post; p = 0.0007)." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 22)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: nurse-reported satisfaction with the process of interpreting/communicating with LEP patients, captured as a single 5-point Likert survey item.

> "Sample pre-survey items included the following: (1) I am satisfied with the current process of communicating with LEP patients/families... Nurses responded using a standard 5-point Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree, 2 = disagree, 3 = neutral, 4 = agree, 5 = strongly agree)." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 20)

### How?

Single-group pre-post survey design evaluating the [[ART - Interactive pictorial communication board (pediatric surgery center)]]; paired pre- and post-implementation nurse responses compared with Wilcoxon rank-sum tests in R.

> "A pre-post survey method was utilized to evaluate the implementation of the interactive communication board." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 20)

> "Wilcoxon rank-sum tests were performed to determine statistical significance between pre- and post-survey staff nurse responses." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 22)

### Who?

Staff nurses at a single large, urban pediatric ambulatory (three-OR) surgery center; surveys sent to all 17 nurses, with 15 completing the pre-survey (88%) and 14 the post-survey (82%).

> "Surveys were distributed to all surgery center staff nurses (n = 17). Pre-surveys were completed by 88% of nurses (n = 15), and 82% of nurses completed the post-survey (n = 14)." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 22)
