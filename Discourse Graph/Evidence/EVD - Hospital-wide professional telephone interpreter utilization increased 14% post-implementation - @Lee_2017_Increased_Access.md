## Description

Overall utilization of professional telephone interpreters across the entire medical center — including floors not exposed to the bedside phone intervention — increased by 14% in the post- vs. pre-implementation period. This is a facility-level utilization measure reported descriptively (no significance test given). The authors note the increase may have been even greater on the intervention floors, though they could not confirm this.

> "Overall utilization of professional telephone interpreters throughout the medical center, including floors not exposed to the intervention, increased by 14% during the post- vs. pre-implementation periods." (Lee, 2017, p. 866)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: overall (facility-level) volume of professional telephone interpreter utilization throughout the medical center.

> "Overall utilization of professional telephone interpreters throughout the medical center, including floors not exposed to the intervention, increased by 14% during the post- vs. pre-implementation periods." (Lee, 2017, p. 866)

### How?

Comparison of hospital-wide professional telephone interpreter utilization between the pre- and post-implementation periods spanning the bedside interpreter phone rollout ([[ART - Bedside interpreter phone system]]); reported as a descriptive percentage change.

> "In this study, we again observed an increase in overall interpreter phone use throughout the hospital, and while we cannot be sure, this increase may have been even greater on the intervention floors." (Lee, 2017, p. 868)

### Who?

The entire academic medical center, including hospital floors not part of the three study floors and not directly exposed to the bedside phone intervention.

> "Overall utilization of professional telephone interpreters throughout the medical center, including floors not exposed to the intervention, increased by 14%" (Lee, 2017, p. 866)

## Other Notes

Because the measure spans non-intervention floors as well, it reflects a hospital-wide shift concurrent with the rollout rather than an isolated intervention-floor effect.
