abstract: 'Objective: This study aimed to examine the proportion of U.S. mental health
  facilities that provide non-English language services.

  Methods: Using data from the 2018 National Mental Health Services Survey, the authors
  examined differences between mental health facilities that provided non-English
  language services and those that did not across 23 organizational characteristics. ^evd-7
  Further analyses compared facilities by their method of language service provision.

  Results: Of 7,503 facilities, 5,186 (69.1%) provided non-English language services.
  These facilities were more likely than others to have high patient volume, be publicly
  owned, and be located in the 10 states with the highest percentage of residents
  with limited English proficiency. Among facilities with language services, 592 (11.4%)
  relied on multilingual staff, 2,532 (48.8%) relied on external on-call interpreters,
  and 2,062 (39.8%) relied on both.

  Conclusions: Most mental health facilities provide non-English language services,
  and those that do tend to be large public organizations located in areas of greatest
  need.
