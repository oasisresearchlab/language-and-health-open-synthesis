title: "Enhancing Dental Care for Patients With Limited English Proficiency: Integrating Interpretation, Cultural Competency, and Artificial Intelligence"
journal: Journal of dental education
pubmed_id: 40411237
abstract: |
  Limited English proficiency (LEP) presents substantial barriers in dental settings, including miscommunication, reduced access to care, and ethical challenges in informed consent. This manuscript explores how professional interpretation, cultural competency training, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools can address these disparities. Through a comprehensive literature review, we evaluate communication platforms, policy mandates, and technological solutions while outlining the ethical considerations related to AI integration. Our findings suggest that leveraging trained interpreters, enhancing provider training in cultural competency, and ensuring ethical AI use can significantly improve outcomes for LEP patients. The paper also highlights the adverse effects of reduced diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) support and proposes actionable strategies for future practice.
keywords: |
  DEI; artificial intelligence; cultural competency; dental ethics; informed consent; interpretation services; limited English proficiency.
specialty: Dental
outcomes: |
  Addressing the challenges faced by LEP patients in dentistry requires a multifaceted approach incorporating interpretation services, AI-powered solutions, and cultural competency training. Implementing professional medical interpreters, increasing the number of bilingual healthcare providers, and leveraging technology can significantly improve health outcomes. AI has the potential to revolutionize dental care for LEP populations by making communication more seamless and reducing disparities. In addition, fostering cultural awareness among healthcare providers and policymakers is crucial for creating an inclusive and equitable healthcare system. Healthcare institutions must invest in these strategies to reduce disparities and enhance patient experiences for LEP populations. Ongoing research and policy development are necessary to refine best practices and optimize healthcare delivery for diverse communities.
intervention: "AI and digital technologies will continue to shape the future of dental care for LEP patients by enhancing accessibility, improving patient–provider communication, and reducing disparities. Recommendations for further advancements include:
Expanding AI-driven interpretation systems in dental clinics
Developing personalized AI-powered chatbots for patient support
Implementing mandatory cultural competency training for dental professionals
Enhancing federal and institutional policies to mandate AI and interpretation services in dentistry
Incorporating translated consent forms into official patient records
Ensuring transparency around AI data usage and privacy
Acknowledging the detrimental effects of reduced DEI funding on LEP services"
