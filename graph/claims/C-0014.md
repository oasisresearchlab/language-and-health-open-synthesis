# C-0014 — LEP patients can achieve medication adherence comparable to or better than English-speaking patients when care is language-concordant

## Other Notes

LEP patients (notably Nepali-speaking refugees served by a concordant bilingual physician) matched or exceeded English-preference patients on adherence measures.

> "LEP patients could have improved outcomes in their adherence to medications by having providers who speak their language or use an interpreter." (Kahler, 2022, p. 31)
