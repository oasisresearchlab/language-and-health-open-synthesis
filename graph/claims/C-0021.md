# C-0021 — A medical Spanish curriculum for resident physicians increases language-concordant encounters and reduces reliance on interpreter phones

## Other Notes

Training providers as bilingual/dual-role clinicians shifts care toward direct language-concordant communication and away from interpreter-phone use, which the authors describe as slower, lower-quality, and impersonal. This is a single-program, non-randomized comparison, so the claim remains tentative.

> "resident physicians feel that becoming proficient in medical Spanish improves their efficiency in the ED as they save time by not having to use interpreter phones" (Stoneking, 2016)
