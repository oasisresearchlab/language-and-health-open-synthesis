# C-0055 — More frequent professional interpretation narrows the LEP-EP gap in pediatric postoperative pain management

More frequent use of professional interpreters narrows the gap in postoperative pain management between children of LEP parents and children of English-proficient parents: LEP children whose families receive interpretation more often have better-controlled pain and analgesic treatment closer to that of English-proficient children.
