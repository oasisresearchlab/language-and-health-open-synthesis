# C-0056 — Language discordance is associated with higher hospital readmission after spine and orthopedic surgery

Patient-surgeon language discordance (limited English proficiency) is associated with higher rates of hospital readmission after spine and orthopedic surgery, even after matching and multivariable adjustment for demographic, comorbidity, and operative factors.
