# C-0062 — Enrolling LEP arthroplasty patients in a multilingual chatbot is associated with fewer hospital readmissions

Enrolling limited-English-proficiency arthroplasty patients in a multilingual perioperative chatbot is associated with fewer hospital readmissions after total joint arthroplasty, relative to comparable LEP patients not enrolled.
