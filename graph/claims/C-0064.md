# C-0064 — Enrolling LEP arthroplasty patients in a multilingual chatbot reduces reoperations

Enrolling limited-English-proficiency arthroplasty patients in a multilingual perioperative chatbot reduces reoperations, relative to comparable LEP patients not enrolled.

## Narrative synthesis

The one study bearing on this claim (Rainey 2023) found no difference in reoperations (0% vs 1.5%, P = 1.000) with a single event across both arms and no power for this outcome. The null is wired as *contradicting*. This thin claim exists mainly to carry the null faithfully; a human may merge or retire it.
