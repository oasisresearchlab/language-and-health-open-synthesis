# C-0073 — The modality of language interpretation influences regional anesthesia utilization

The modality through which language interpretation is delivered (in-person versus telephone versus video) influences downstream clinical decisions such as regional anesthesia utilization. Sabra et al. raised this as an open question and, in the interpreter-using arthroplasty subgroup, found no association between interpreter modality and RA use.
