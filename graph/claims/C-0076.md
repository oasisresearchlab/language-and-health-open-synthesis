# C-0076 — Electronic consent systems with auto-populated translated templates deliver language-concordant written consent to most LEP patients whose language is supported

Electronic consent systems that store translated consent templates and auto-populate them in the patient's recorded preferred language deliver a language-concordant written consent form to most LEP patients whose preferred language has a supported template.
