# C-0079 — Limited English proficiency is associated with longer hospital length of stay after total joint arthroplasty

Limited English proficiency (patient-clinician language discordance) is associated with a longer hospital length of stay after total joint arthroplasty, independent of demographic, comorbidity, and operative factors.
