# C-0080 — Limited English proficiency is associated with higher hospitalization cost after total joint arthroplasty

Limited English proficiency (patient-clinician language discordance) is associated with higher total hospitalization cost after total joint arthroplasty, independent of demographic, comorbidity, and operative factors.
