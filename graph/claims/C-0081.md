# C-0081 — Limited English proficiency is associated with increased discharge to a skilled care facility after total joint arthroplasty

Limited English proficiency (patient-clinician language discordance) is associated with an increased likelihood of discharge to a skilled care facility (rather than home) after total joint arthroplasty, independent of demographic, comorbidity, and operative factors.
