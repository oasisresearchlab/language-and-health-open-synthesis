# C-0087 — Limited English proficiency is not associated with intraoperative or postoperative complication rates in cataract surgery

Limited English proficiency is not associated with higher intraoperative or postoperative complication rates, nor with longer operative time, in cataract surgery; despite more complex cases, LEP patients do not experience a higher complication rate.
