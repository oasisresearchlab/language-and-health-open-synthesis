# C-0088 — LEP cataract patients carry a higher diabetes and diabetic-eye-disease comorbidity burden

Patients with limited English proficiency undergoing cataract surgery carry a higher burden of type 2 diabetes and diabetic eye disease (macular edema, diabetic retinopathy) than English-proficient patients.
