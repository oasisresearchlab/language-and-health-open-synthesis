# C-0090 — LEP patients are less likely to undergo Nd-YAG laser capsulotomy after cataract surgery

Patients with limited English proficiency are less likely than English-proficient patients to undergo subsequent Nd:YAG laser capsulotomy after cataract surgery, a disparity in access to follow-up treatment for posterior capsule opacification rather than an indicator of lower need.
