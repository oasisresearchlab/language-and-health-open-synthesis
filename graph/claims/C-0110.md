# C-0110 — LEP patients have low uptake of patient portals for electronic consent delivery and signing

Patients with limited English proficiency have low uptake of patient portals for electronic consent delivery and signing, limiting the reach of portal-based flexibility (remote review, household involvement) that eConsent could otherwise provide.
