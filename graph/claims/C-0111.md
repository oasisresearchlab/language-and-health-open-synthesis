# C-0111 — Children from LEP families experience a longer time to first analgesia after surgery than children from English-proficient families

Hospitalized children from limited-English-proficiency (LEP) families experience a longer time to first analgesia after surgery, and are less likely to receive any analgesia in the early postoperative window, than children from English-proficient families — a disparity that persists after adjustment for demographic and clinical confounders.
