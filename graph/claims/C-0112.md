# C-0112 — LEP is not associated with the timing or amount of postoperative opioid analgesia in hospitalized children

Among hospitalized postsurgical children, limited English proficiency is not associated with the timing (time to first opioid), proportion receiving opioids, or cumulative dose of postoperative opioid analgesia — the disparity is specific to overall/early analgesia rather than opioids.
