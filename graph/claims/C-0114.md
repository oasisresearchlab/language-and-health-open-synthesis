# C-0114 — LEP is not associated with the amount of nonopioid analgesia administered to hospitalized children within 24 hours of surgery

Among hospitalized postsurgical children, limited English proficiency is not associated with the number of nonopioid analgesic doses (acetaminophen, ketorolac, or ibuprofen) administered within 24 hours after surgery.
