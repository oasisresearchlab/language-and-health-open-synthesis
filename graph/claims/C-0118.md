# C-0118 — Many LEP surgical patients receive infrequent interpreting services during peri-operative admission

Many surgical patients with limited English proficiency receive interpreting services infrequently during peri-operative admission — often less than once daily — indicating under-provision of language-access services relative to the level of communication such patients need.
