# C-0121 — When comprehensive professional interpreter services are readily available most LEP patients use them rather than family or ad hoc interpreters

When comprehensive professional interpreter services are readily available (and family-interpreter use is discouraged by policy), the large majority of LEP patients use the professional services, and reliance on family members or other ad hoc interpreters becomes uncommon.
