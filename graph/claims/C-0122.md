# C-0122 — In-person interpreting is the predominant modality used for LEP surgical patients with telephone and video as secondary options

Where an institution staffs on-site interpreters, in-person interpreting is the predominant modality used for LEP surgical and procedural patients, with telephone and video remote interpreting serving secondary, backup roles.
