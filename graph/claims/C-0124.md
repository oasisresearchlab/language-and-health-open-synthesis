# C-0124 — Providing comprehensive professional interpreter services imposes substantial financial and staffing burdens on health care institutions

Providing comprehensive professional interpreter services — especially maintaining on-staff in-person interpreters across many languages — imposes substantial and largely unreimbursed financial and staffing burdens on health care institutions.
