# C-0125 — Patients who need language assistance report lower postoperative pain intensity than English-proficient surgical patients

Patients who need language assistance services (limited English proficiency) report lower postoperative pain intensity than English-proficient patients undergoing similar surgery — a difference that, where statistically significant, tends to be small and of uncertain clinical relevance, and that may not persist after multivariable adjustment.
