# C-0132 — Translated written patient information booklets alone are insufficient for most LEP patients to comprehend key cancer treatment information

Providing translated written patient information booklets alone — even accessibility-designed, professionally translated ones — is insufficient for most LEP patients to comprehend key cancer treatment information, implicating health literacy and other communication barriers beyond translation availability.
