# C-0134 — Bilingual interlinear consent forms improve LEP patient comprehension of treatment intent during interpreted consent consultations

Providing a bilingual (interlinear) consent form alongside verbal interpretation improves LEP patients' comprehension of treatment intent during interpreted informed-consent consultations, compared with a conventional English-only consent form.
