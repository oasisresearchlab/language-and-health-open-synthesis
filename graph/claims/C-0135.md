# C-0135 — Bilingual interlinear consent forms increase LEP patient self-rated confidence and understanding during interpreted consent

Providing a bilingual (interlinear) consent form increases LEP patients' self-rated confidence in their understanding during interpreted informed-consent consultations, compared with a conventional English-only consent form.
