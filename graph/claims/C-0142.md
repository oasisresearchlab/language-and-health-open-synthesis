# C-0142 — Among adequately interpreted LEP patients, the type of interpretive service does not significantly affect procedural consent comprehension

Among LEP patients who receive adequate language assistance, the specific modality of interpretive service (bilingual provider vs professional interpreter vs staff acting as interpreter) does not significantly change their comprehension of procedural informed consent — though studies to date are underpowered to rule out modest differences.
