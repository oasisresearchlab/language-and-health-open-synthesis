# C-0144 — Family interpreter proxies are a reliable alternative to professional interpreters for administering patient-reported outcome surveys to LEP patients

Family or carer interpreter proxies produce patient-reported outcome survey results that agree closely enough with those obtained through certified professional healthcare interpreters to be a reliable, lower-cost alternative for routine post-discharge surveillance of LEP patients, with negligible systematic bias at the group level.
