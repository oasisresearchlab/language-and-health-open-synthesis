# C-0147 — Language-concordant patient education resources help lessen surgical-access disparities for LEP patients

Familiarity with and provision of language-concordant patient education resources (e.g., translated or machine-translated educational materials) is a practical lever that helps lessen multifactorial surgical-access disparities for patients with limited English proficiency.
