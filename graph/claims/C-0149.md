# C-0149 — Professional interpreter use improves satisfaction, quality of care, and outcomes for LEP patients

Use of professional interpreters in language-discordant clinical encounters positively affects patient satisfaction, quality of care, and outcomes for patients with limited English proficiency, and they should be preferred over ad hoc arrangements.
