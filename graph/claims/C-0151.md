# C-0151 — Language-concordant communication for LEP surgical patients is especially deficient at hospital discharge

Language-concordant communication for surgical patients with limited English proficiency is especially deficient at hospital discharge, where documented interpreter use and provision of language-concordant written materials are markedly lower than earlier in the admission.
