# C-0152 — Documented professional interpreter use for LEP surgical inpatients is broad in reach but low in frequency and timeliness

Although most surgical inpatients with limited English proficiency have at least one professional interpreter encounter documented during their admission, documented interpreter use is low in frequency and not consistently timely, with only a minority receiving interpretation early in the stay.
