# C-0153 — Preferred-language group is associated with the likelihood that LEP surgical patients receive early interpreter services

Among surgical patients with limited English proficiency, the specific preferred-language group is associated with the likelihood of receiving early (first-24-hour) professional interpreter services, with more common language groups (e.g., Spanish, Chinese) more likely to receive timely interpretation than patients speaking less common languages.
