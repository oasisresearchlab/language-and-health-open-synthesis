# C-0154 — Patient demographic and insurance characteristics are not associated with interpreter use among LEP surgical patients

Among surgical patients with limited English proficiency, patient demographic and insurance characteristics — race and ethnicity, gender, age, and insurance status — are not associated with the likelihood of professional interpreter use.
