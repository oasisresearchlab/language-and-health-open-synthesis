# C-0155 — Multiple discrete factors influence whether partially bilingual physicians use their own language skills or call an interpreter

A variety of discrete, identifiable factors — spanning physician knowledge, clinical complexity, interpreter access and quality, self-assessment of language skill, encounter efficiency, interpersonal rapport, and cost — influence whether a partially bilingual physician relies on his or her own non-English language skills or calls a trained interpreter, and these factors carry patient-safety implications when the wrong path is chosen.
