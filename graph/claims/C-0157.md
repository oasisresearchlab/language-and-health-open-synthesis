# C-0157 — Cost of interpreter services is a comparatively minor driver of the interpreter-use decision

Relative to factors such as physician knowledge, clinical complexity, and interpreter access, the cost or inadequate reimbursement of interpreter services is a comparatively minor driver of whether partially bilingual physicians call an interpreter — though it can become an overriding concern in less well-resourced or rural practice settings.
