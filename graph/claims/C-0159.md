# C-0159 — Professional interpreters enable LEP patients to disclose clinically critical information that family or ad hoc interpreters miss

Using professional medical interpreters enables patients with limited English proficiency to disclose clinically critical information — including safety-critical findings such as suicidal ideation — that reliance on family members or ad hoc interpreters would miss, improving the accuracy of clinical assessment.
