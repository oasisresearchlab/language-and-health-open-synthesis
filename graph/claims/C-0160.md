# C-0160 — Assistive listening devices restore effective communication for hearing-impaired perioperative patients

Providing assistive listening devices (e.g., amplifiers with earpieces) at the bedside restores effective patient–provider communication for hearing-impaired patients in perioperative settings where their own hearing aids are unavailable.
