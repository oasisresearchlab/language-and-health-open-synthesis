# C-0161 — Language concordance increases patient question-asking during pediatric surgery clinic visits

Patient–provider language concordance increases the number of patient-initiated questions during a clinical visit, expanding access to information; interpreter-mediated (discordant) care is associated with fewer questions asked, and the effect persists after adjustment for education, native language, and new-patient status.
