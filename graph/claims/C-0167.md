# C-0167 — Nurse uptake of communication boards stays low even when reported satisfaction is high

Real-world uptake of a pictorial communication board remains low — well below high-performance compliance benchmarks — even when nurses report high satisfaction with and acceptability of the tool, reflecting a gap between attitudinal endorsement and actual utilization that is partly driven by usability barriers and the availability of alternative interpreting options.
