# C-0172 — Pictorial communication boards reduce nurses' perceived time and effort to interpret routine nursing tasks

A pictorial communication board reduces the time and effort nurses perceive is required to interpret and communicate routine perioperative nursing tasks for limited-English-proficiency (LEP) patients, by handling routine exchanges without invoking a formal interpreter.
