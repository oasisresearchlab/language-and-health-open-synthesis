# C-0178 — Language-access demand concentrates in a few dominant languages with a long regionally variable tail that is hardest to serve

Beyond a few dominant languages (led by Spanish), language-access demand extends into a long tail of less-common languages that varies significantly by geographic region, and the less-common languages are disproportionately the hardest for health systems to serve.
