# C-0179 — Health systems frequently rely on ad hoc family and friend interpreters for LEP patients despite guidelines against the practice

Health systems frequently rely on ad hoc, non-qualified interpreters (patients' family members and friends) to communicate with LEP patients, despite guidelines and best-practice recommendations against the practice.
