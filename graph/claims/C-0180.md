# C-0180 — Health systems face substantial provider- and system-level barriers to meeting the language-access needs of LEP patients

Health systems face substantial provider-level barriers (inability to locate translated resources or interpreters, unavailable bilingual staff) and system-level barriers (absent or outdated language-preference tracking, inadequate documentation and training) that impede meeting the language-access needs of LEP patients.
