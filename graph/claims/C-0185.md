# C-0185 — Even with professional interpreters information is lost in translation undermining accurate patient-provider understanding

Even when a professional interpreter is used, information is lost in translation, leaving providers uncertain that accurate, mutual patient-provider understanding has been achieved; ensuring communication fidelity remains a barrier to LEP care beyond mere interpreter access.
