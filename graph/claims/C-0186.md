# C-0186 — Collecting PROMs from LEP patients imposes additional provider time and resource burden that leads to PROMs being skipped

Collecting patient-reported outcome measures from patients with limited English proficiency imposes additional provider and staff time and resource burden compared with English-speaking patients, and this burden leads clinicians to skip or abandon PROMs in the clinic workflow — especially when relying on phone or video interpreters.
