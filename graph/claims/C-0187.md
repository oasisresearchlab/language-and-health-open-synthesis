# C-0187 — Providers prefer in-person interpreters over phone or video interpreters for communicating with and collecting PROMs from LEP patients

Providers prefer in-person interpreters over telephone or video interpreters for communicating with LEP patients and collecting PROMs, because in-person interpreters can read emotion, mood, and body language, help with forms, and build relationships over time, whereas remote interpreters miss these nonverbal cues.
