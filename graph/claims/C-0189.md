# C-0189 — Institutional leadership and funding support are key facilitators for developing and implementing validated multilingual PROMs

Institutional and departmental leadership advocacy, together with the financial/funding support it unlocks, is a key facilitator for developing and implementing validated multilingual PROMs — while its absence, particularly at low-capital private and safety-net institutions, is a barrier.
