# C-0190 — Word-for-word translation of PROMs without cultural and linguistic validation is not sufficient for valid measurement in LEP populations

Simple word-for-word translation of a patient-reported outcome instrument, without cultural and linguistic validation of its constructs in the target population, is not sufficient for valid measurement in LEP populations, because direct translation loses the cultural and linguistic nuances the instrument was developed to capture.
