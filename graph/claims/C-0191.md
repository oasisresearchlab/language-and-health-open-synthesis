# C-0191 — Availability of validated translated PROMs in the patients preferred language is the primary determinant of PROM collection for LEP patients

The availability of a validated translation of the instrument in the patient's preferred language is the primary determinant of whether patient-reported outcome measures can be collected from patients with limited English proficiency: with valid translations collection is feasible, and without them it becomes a barrier.
