# C-0192 — Lack of multilingual electronic medical record integration is a barrier to PROM collection for LEP patients

Lack of multilingual electronic medical record integration is a barrier to PROM collection for LEP patients: because EMR content is delivered only in English, EMR-embedded PROMs cannot be completed by LEP patients without an interpreter, and paper-collected PROMs go unintegrated.
