# C-0196 — Machine translation with human postediting lowers the cost of translating health materials versus fully human translation

Machine translation with human postediting lowers the cost of translating health materials compared with fully human (vendor) translation, chiefly by eliminating the vendor's charge for the initial translation while retaining only human postediting labor.
