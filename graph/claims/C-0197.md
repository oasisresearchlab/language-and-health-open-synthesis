# C-0197 — Unsupervised machine translation fails to meet professional-quality standards for medical safety information in most non-English languages

Unsupervised free machine translation (e.g., Google Translate) fails to reach professional-quality translation standards for medical safety information across most non-English languages, with a large fraction of output containing meaning-changing errors.
