# C-0201 — Machine translation of patient-education material into a high-resource language does not introduce more clinically dangerous errors than professional translation

Unsupervised machine translation of patient-education material into a high-resource language (e.g. Spanish) does not introduce more serious, clinically dangerous errors than professional human translation.
