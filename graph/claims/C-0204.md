# C-0204 — Public-health translation is under-resourced and performed as an intermittent add-on rather than a scheduled function

Translation of health-promotion materials in public-health agencies is under-resourced and treated as an intermittent, ad hoc add-on task performed on top of staff members' primary duties rather than a scheduled, dedicated function.
