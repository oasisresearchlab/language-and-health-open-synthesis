# C-0205 — Public-health agencies lack standardized policies and clear guidelines governing when and what to translate

Public-health agencies lack standardized policies and clear guidelines governing when, what, and for whom to translate, and this absence of guidance is itself a barrier that can prevent translations from being produced at all.
