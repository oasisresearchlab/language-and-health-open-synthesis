# C-0206 — Public-health professionals perceive publicly available machine translation as too low-quality to use unsupervised for health materials

Public-health professionals perceive publicly available machine translation as too low-quality to use unsupervised for health materials, fearing that its literal, error-prone output could harm health messaging and damage the agency's credibility with the community.
