# C-0207 — Machine translation of public-health materials should be followed by human post-editing to reach acceptable quality and be adopted

Machine translation of public-health materials should never be used in isolation but must be followed by human review and post-editing by a native speaker for translations to reach acceptable quality and for the technology to be adopted into public-health practice.
