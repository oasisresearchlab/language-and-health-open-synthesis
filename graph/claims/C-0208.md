# C-0208 — Producing translated public-health materials follows a multi-phase workflow that MT tools must fit into

Producing translated public-health materials follows a multi-phase information workflow spanning material development, translation, quality assurance, and post-translation distribution, and machine-translation tools must be designed to fit into this existing workflow rather than displace it.
