# C-0209 — A voice-enabled machine translation app matches professional human translators on simple patient-education sentences but makes more errors as sentence complexity increases

A voice-enabled mobile machine-translation app produces translation quality comparable to professional human translators for simple, short patient-education sentences, but makes more errors as sentence complexity increases.
