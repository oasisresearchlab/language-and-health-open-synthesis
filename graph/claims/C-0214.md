# C-0214 — Human professional translation is preferred over machine translation with postediting for quality of translated public-health materials

For translated public-health materials, expert raters prefer professional human translation over machine translation with human postediting (MT+PE) on translation quality, especially for linguistically divergent language pairs such as English-Chinese.
