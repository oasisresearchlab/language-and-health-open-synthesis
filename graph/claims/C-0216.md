# C-0216 — Machine translation of English into Chinese is dominated by word-sense and word-order errors that are the most cognitively demanding to correct

Machine translation from English into Chinese is dominated by word-sense (mistranslated meaning) and word-order errors, the error types that require the most cognitive effort to correct.
