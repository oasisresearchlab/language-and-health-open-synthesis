# C-0217 — Postediting instructions to make all corrections needed rather than only minimal edits improve machine-translation quality

Instructing posteditors to make as many corrections as needed for quality — rather than only minimal necessary edits — improves the quality of machine-translated health materials, at the cost of slower postediting.
