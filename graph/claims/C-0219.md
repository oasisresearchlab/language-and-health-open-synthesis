# C-0219 — Fixed-phrase translation apps are preferred over real-time voice-to-voice translation apps for healthcare communication

For healthcare communication, users prefer fixed-phrase (preset, curated) translation apps over open, real-time voice-to-voice translation apps, because the preset apps are easier to use and avoid the accuracy failures of free voice translation.
