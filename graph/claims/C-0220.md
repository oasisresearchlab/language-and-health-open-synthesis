# C-0220 — Concerns about translation accuracy make mobile translation apps unsuitable for complex or high-risk clinical communication

Because their translation accuracy is unreliable, mobile translation apps are viewed as unsuitable for complex, high-risk, or medically important clinical communication, and are appropriate only for simple, low-risk conversation.
