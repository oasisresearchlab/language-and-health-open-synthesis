# C-0230 — Clinicians report overall satisfaction and ease of use with point-of-care interpretation applications

Clinicians report overall satisfaction with point-of-care interpretation applications, citing ease of use, more streamlined communication with patients, and decreased frustration when communicating with limited-language-proficiency patients.

## Narrative synthesis

Provider (clinician) satisfaction was one of the review's predefined outcome variables. Three of the eight studies reported on provider satisfaction, and the review synthesizes these as overall positive. This is a self-reported satisfaction/usability claim, not an effectiveness claim; the review notes it did not include a formal certainty appraisal. Certainty/GRADE appraisal is left for expert review.

> "Clinicians reported overall satisfaction with the applications due to ease of use" (Kiblinger, 2024, p. 4)
