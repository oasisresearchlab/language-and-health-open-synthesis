# C-0234 — Evaluations of point-of-care interpretation applications center the provider rather than the LLP patient perspective

Existing evaluations of point-of-care interpretation applications focus predominantly on the healthcare provider's perspective, leaving the limited-language-proficiency patient's own perceptions of these tools largely unstudied.

## Narrative synthesis

The review identifies a research gap: most included applications were assessed through the provider's evaluation of the technology, and the LLP patient's own perceptions were rarely captured, prompting a call for future studies of the patient perspective. This is a measurement-scope claim about whose perspective the evidence represents. Certainty/GRADE appraisal is left for expert review.

> "there needs to be additional studies looking at perceptions of these applications from the LLP patient perspective, as most applications in this review focused solely on the healthcare provider's evaluation of the technologies" (Kiblinger, 2024, p. 5)
