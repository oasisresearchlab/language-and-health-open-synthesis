# C-0242 — User satisfaction with translation technology is uniformly high and does not track its measured accuracy or usability

## Narrative synthesis

Self-reported satisfaction with translation technology is uniformly high — 96.6% of LEP patients, 83–87% of nurses, means of 4.7–4.9/5 for S-MINDS, 75% of staff rating apps useful — and does not track measured accuracy or usability. The internal contradiction is the evidence: the same Hwang staff who rated the apps useful and would use them again also reported that Google Translate was slow, needed repeated attempts, and was degraded by dialect and accent — satisfaction and measured performance point opposite ways *within one study*.
