# E-0111 — Initial consultation length did not differ between direct-Spanish and interpreter-services arms (37 vs 33 min)

## Description

The duration of the initial consultation did not differ significantly between the two communication modalities: interpreter-services consultations averaged 33 minutes versus 37 minutes for direct-Spanish consultations (P = .33). Thus the satisfaction and communication-content differences arose without a measurable difference in visit length.

> "When considering the content of the initial patient consultation, we found no signiﬁcant differences in consultation length between study arms, with the average consultation of the interpreter-services arm lasting 33 minutes compared with the direct-Spanish arm lasting 37 minutes (P = .33)." (Seible, 2021, p. 858)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: average length (minutes) of the initial patient-physician consultation, compared between study arms.

> "we found no signiﬁcant differences in consultation length between study arms" (Seible, 2021, p. 858)

### How?

Consultation duration derived from the recorded/transcribed initial-consultation encounters in the content-analysis subset; compared between arms (P = .33).

> "For the ﬁnal 43 enrolled patients, we obtained audio recordings of the initial consultation to enable analysis of communication content." (Seible, 2021, p. 858)

### Who?

The content-analysis subset of initial consultations: interpreter-services n = 19 and direct-Spanish n = 24, within the broader RCT of 83 Spanish-speaking cancer patients.

> "Interpreter services (n = 19) ... Direct Spanish (n = 24)" (Seible, 2021, p. 863)

## Other Notes

Relevant to delivery-cost/effort framing: language-concordant care here delivered higher satisfaction without lengthening the visit.
