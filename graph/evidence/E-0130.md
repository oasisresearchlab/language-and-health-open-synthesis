# E-0130 — LEP and English-primary patients engaged equally with the multilingual chatbot (12.3 vs 12.2 responses, P=.959)

## Description

![](/attachments/Joshua_2023_Multilingual_Chatbot-fig3.png)

Among TJA patients enrolled in the multilingual SMS chatbot, LEP patients responded to a mean of 12.3 ± 16.6 (range 0-80) chatbot messages versus 12.2 ± 12.1 (range 0-106) responses by patients with English as their primary language (EPL) — a difference that was not statistically significant (P = .959) (Fig. 3). The figure further shows no significant engagement difference between the two groups either before or after surgery. Engagement was thus effectively equal across the language groups.

> "On average, LEP patients responded to 12.3 ± 16.6 (range 0-80) SMS chatbot messages in comparison to 12.2 ± 12.1 (range 0-106) message responses by EPL patients, which was not significantly different (P = .959), Figure 3." (Rainey, 2023, p. S79)

> "Number of patient responses to chatbot messages before and after surgery in those patients enrolled in the chatbot service. (No significant difference seen between English speakers and non-English speakers before or after surgery.)" (Rainey, 2023, p. S81, Fig. 3)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: patient engagement, operationalized as the total count of patient-generated text messages sent to the chatbot across the perioperative period.

> "Patient engagement was measured using the total number of patient-generated text messages sent to the chatbot during both the preoperative and postoperative period." (Rainey, 2023, p. S79)

### How?

Retrospective review comparing message-response counts between LEP and EPL patients enrolled in the A-0004, analyzed with independent t-tests.

> "The multilingual SMS chatbot service used in this study was provided by StreaMD (Chicago, IL)." (Rainey, 2023, p. S79)

### Who?

The 1,282 TJA patients (705 knees, 577 hips) enrolled in the chatbot at a single institution (University of Utah) from 2020-2022; of these, 47 received their messages in a language other than English (LEP) and the remainder were EPL.

> "1,282 TJA patient were identified (705 knees, 577 hips), and of these patients, 47 were enrolled to receive their chatbot messages in a language other than English." (Rainey, 2023, p. S79)

## Other Notes

This is a null (no-difference) result and is reported here as such; it supports an *equivalence* claim (LEP engage the chatbot as much as EPL patients), not a superiority claim.
