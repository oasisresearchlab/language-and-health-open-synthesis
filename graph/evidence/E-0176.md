# E-0176 — Higher complex-surgery and CDE rates in LEP persisted after adjustment for mature cataract

## Description

The higher rates of complex surgery and higher CDE in LEP patients were not simply explained by their more mature cataracts: both remained significantly higher among LEP patients in multivariable analysis after adjustment for mature cataract (both P < .0001). This suggests an association between LEP status and surgical complexity independent of cataract maturity.

> "Complex surgery and higher CDE remained significantly higher among LEP patients after adjustment for mature cataract (both P values <0.0001)." (Zafar, 2023, p. 596)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: complex-surgery classification and CDE, each modeled as an outcome adjusted for mature cataract.

> "Comparisons between LEP and EP for complex surgery and CDE were also adjusted for mature cataracts in multivariable analyses." (Zafar, 2023, p. 596)

### How?

Multivariable analysis comparing LEP vs EP for complex surgery and CDE with adjustment for mature cataract, within the GEE framework used for LEP-status comparisons.

> "Comparisons between LEP and EP for complex surgery and CDE were also adjusted for mature cataracts in multivariable analyses." (Zafar, 2023, p. 596)

### Who?

The full retrospective cohort of 13,590 phacoemulsification eyes at a single academic eye center; 868 (6.4%) LEP vs 12,722 EP.

> "A total of 13 590 eyes were included in the analytic database. Of these, 868 (6.4%) were identified from LEP patients." (Zafar, 2023, p. 596)
