# E-0296 — Language-discordant families more often reported their question-asking was limited by a language barrier (beta=0.892, p=0.001)

## Description

![](/attachments/Jaramillo_2016_Hispanic_Clinic-table5.png)

In the OLS survey model, language-discordant participants significantly more often reported that they desired to ask more questions but were limited by a language barrier (language-concordance β = 0.892, p = 0.001) (Table 5). This self-report supplies a plausible mechanism for the observed reduction in patient-initiated questions in discordant visits: the barrier itself suppressed question-asking rather than a lack of desire to ask.

> "Language discordant participants reported their desire to ask more questions but were limited by language barriers (p = 0.001)." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 672)

> "Language discordant participants reported that they desired to ask more questions but were limited by a language barrier (p = 0.001)." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 670)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: the participant's 5-point Likert rating on the "question asking limited by language barrier" survey item.

> "Participants indicated their level of agreement with a series of 15 questions using a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (strongly disagree/very negative/never) to 5 (strongly agree/very positive/always)." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 672)

### How?

A surgery-specific post-visit satisfaction survey analyzed by OLS regression using language concordance and patient's language as explanatory variables. The discordant arm experienced interpreter-mediated care.

> "Regression modeling was used to determine the factors associated with survey results using language concordance and patient's language as explanatory variables." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 672)

### Who?

The 156-family cohort of pediatric surgery patients at a single academic children's hospital, surveyed after their visit.

> "A total of 156 families were enrolled including 57 concordant-English, 52 LEP concordant-Spanish and 47 LEP discordant families." (Jaramillo, 2016, p. 672)

## Other Notes

Table 5 "Question asking limited by language barrier" coefficients: language concordance 0.892 (p = 0.001) and patient's language −0.229 (p = 0.393, non-significant).
