# E-0297 — Nurses reported difficulty using the board when the English word was absent under the translation

## Description

Free-text responses on the post-implementation survey surfaced a concrete usability barrier: nurses had difficulty using the board when the English word was not printed under the translated word, forcing them to flip through the boards to locate the English version. Nurses suggested adding an English translation directly below the other language to improve ease of use and efficiency — a design limitation that plausibly contributed to the low overall utilization.

> "Responses indicated that nurses had difficulty utilizing the board when the English word was not included under the translated word." (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 22)

> "One nurse reported, “It would be more helpful if there was an English translation directly below the other language, just for ease of understanding for staff. It also would make communication more efficient instead of flipping through the boards looking for the English version.”" (Kiblinger, 2022, p. 22)
