# E-0353 — The leading reason staff did not use a translation app was that a family member was available (16 of 24, 66.6%)

## Description

![](/attachments/Hwang_2022_Testing_use-table1.png)
On the end-of-trial staff survey, the most frequently reported reason a translation app was not used was that a family member or other person was available to help with communication (16/24, 66.6%), ahead of an interpreter being available (10/24, 41.6%), it being easier to "get by" (8/24, 33.3%), or being under time pressure (8/24, 33.3%) (Table 1). The discussion notes reliance on the family member represents a breach of government and/or hospital interpreter policy.

> "For most respondents, if translation apps were not used it was because a family member was present to help with communication (16/24, 66.6%)." (Hwang, 2022, p. 581)

> "for most respondents, if translation apps were not used, it was because a family member was present, which represents a breach of government and/or hospital policy." (Hwang, 2022, p. 583)

## Methods Context

### What?

The observable: staff-reported reasons for not using a translation app in a given interaction, recorded as selectable barrier categories.

> "and barriers to using translation apps" (Hwang, 2022, p. 580)

### How?

A self-report staff survey administered at the conclusion of the two-month trial, tabulated with descriptive statistics.

> "staff were invited to complete a staff survey measuring their experience of using the translation app, estimated frequency of use, most frequently used phrases, enablers, and barriers to using translation apps" (Hwang, 2022, p. 580)

### Who?

The 24 nursing and allied health staff across four aged-care hospital wards at three Melbourne hospital sites who completed the survey (2 missing on this item).

> "Twenty-­four nursing and allied health staff across the four wards completed the staff survey." (Hwang, 2022, p. 581)

## Other Notes

This finding bears on reliance on ad hoc family interpreters as the default fallback in everyday care; family presence, not app or interpreter unavailability, was the leading reason the trialed apps were not used.
